## Answer Template

For each question, document:

1. **Short answer** (30 seconds)
2. **Deep answer** (2–3 minutes)
3. **Production example** from your work or lab projects
4. **Follow-up questions** interviewers often ask
5. **Code snippet** (if applicable)

## Weekly Interview Prep

- **Saturday:** 10 new questions + revise 20 old ones
- **Before interviews:** Run through `notes/interview.md` cheat sheet
